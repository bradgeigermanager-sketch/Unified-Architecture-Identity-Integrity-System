11. Full Ontology Definitions
The ontology is the backbone of the entire system. Below are the **complete definitions** for each class, including attributes, relationships, and constraints.

---

## 🧩 11.1 Identity  
Represents a person or entity whose records are being analyzed.

### Attributes  
- **identityId** — unique identifier  
- **identityType** — person, organization, synthetic candidate, emergent identity  
- **attributes** — demographic, behavioral, lifecycle metadata  
- **records** — list of Record objects  
- **anomalies** — list of Anomaly objects  
- **continuityMarkers** — lifecycle markers (renewals, filings, updates)  

### Relationships  
- Identity → Record (`hasRecord`)  
- Identity → Anomaly (`hasAnomaly`)  
- Identity → Domain (`participatesInDomain`)  
- Identity → Goal (`hasGoal`)  

---

## 🧩 11.2 Record  
Represents a domain‑specific data object.

### Attributes  
- **recordId**  
- **domain**  
- **recordType**  
- **attributes**  
- **provenance**  
- **timestamp**  
- **confidenceScore**  

### Relationships  
- Record → Provenance (`hasProvenance`)  
- Record → Domain (`belongsToDomain`)  
- Record → Identity (`attachedToIdentity`)  

---

## 🧩 11.3 Domain

- Travel  
- Licensing

- **rules**

---

## 🧩 11.4 Anomaly

- Emergent Identity  
- Spatiotemporal Cluster  

### Attributes  
- **anomalyId**  
- **anomalyType**  
- **severity**  
- **attributes**  
- **relatedRecords**  
- **relatedDomains**
