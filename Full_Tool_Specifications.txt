18. Full Tool Specifications  
Tools are defined with purpose, inputs, outputs, and integration points.

---

## 🧩 18.1 Property Normalizer  
- **Purpose:** Normalize property records  
- **Inputs:** raw property records  
- **Outputs:** normalized property records  
- **Integration:** record collection engine  

---

## 🧩 18.2 Utility Normalizer  
- **Purpose:** Normalize utility records  
- **Inputs:** raw utility records  
- **Outputs:** normalized utility records  

---

## 🧩 18.3 Communications Normalizer  
- **Purpose:** Normalize communications billing  
- **Inputs:** raw billing records  
- **Outputs:** normalized billing records  

---

## 🧩 18.4 Reconciliation Tool  
- **Purpose:** Resolve mismatches  
- **Inputs:** conflicting records  
- **Outputs:** reconciled records  

---

## 🧩 18.5 Provenance Updater  
- **Purpose:** Update provenance metadata  
- **Inputs:** records  
- **Outputs:** updated provenance  

---

## 🧩 18.6 Continuity Validator  
- **Purpose:** Validate lifecycle continuity  
- **Inputs:** continuity markers  
- **Outputs:** continuity validation results
